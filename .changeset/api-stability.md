Documented what semver covers: `outlineSvg`, `outlinePathData`, `outlineSvgToMultiPolygon`, their options, the warnings and the CLI are the stable API. The lower-level exports (`parseSvg`, `flattenSegments`, `strokePolyline`, `unionRings`, `fitRing` and friends) follow the engine and may change in a minor release, with a changelog entry.
